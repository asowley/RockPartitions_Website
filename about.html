About Rock Partitions

A West Midlands drylining, metal stud partition and ceilings company.

Rock Partitions is owner-led, with a scalable team of highly skilled professionals providing the range of expertise needed to deliver the best possible service in a time-sensitive industry.

Approach

Scalable, professional support for builders, plastering companies and refurbishment projects.

We are a West Midlands based drylining, metal stud and ceilings company. Our team is scalable, allowing us to deliver a reliable service across projects of different sizes and timescales.

We work with local plastering companies, big and small, to help deliver a professional service. We also work with builders who require drylining and partitioning services, from new build homes through to full renovations of older buildings into flats or offices.
