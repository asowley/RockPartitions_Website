How Rock Partitions handles enquiries and website information.

Plain English notice

Privacy notice

Who we are

Rock Partitions is a West Midlands based drylining, metal stud partition and ceilings company. For privacy enquiries, contact Rock Partitions by WhatsApp or phone on +44 7775 029205.

What information we collect

This website does not include a contact form or account area. If you contact Rock Partitions through WhatsApp, phone or another direct method, you may choose to provide your name, phone number, project location, enquiry details, drawings, photos, measurements or other information needed to discuss the work.

How we use information

Information is used to respond to enquiries, discuss projects, arrange site visits, prepare quotes, plan work, keep project records and manage customer or supplier relationships.

Our lawful basis

We usually use personal information because it is needed to take steps before entering into a contract, to carry out agreed work, or because Rock Partitions has a legitimate interest in responding to enquiries and running the business.

Who information may be shared with

Information is only shared where needed, for example with trusted self-employed labourers, subcontractors, suppliers, professional advisers, accountants, insurers, payment providers or where required by law. Rock Partitions does not sell personal information.

How long information is kept

Enquiry and project information is kept only for as long as needed for the enquiry, project, business records, tax/accounting requirements, legal obligations or to handle any follow-up questions.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or object to how it is used, subject to legal and business record requirements. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office if you are unhappy with how your information is handled.

WhatsApp and external services

If you use a WhatsApp link, you will leave this website and use WhatsApp's service. WhatsApp handles information under its own privacy terms. If this website is hosted on a platform such as GitHub Pages, the hosting provider may process basic technical information, such as server logs, to deliver and secure the website.

Cookie notice

Cookies used by this website

This website does not currently set cookies, use analytics cookies, run advertising pixels or use tracking scripts. Because no non-essential cookies are used, a cookie consent banner is not shown.

If this changes

If analytics, embedded media, maps, advertising pixels or other tracking tools are added in future, this notice should be updated and a suitable consent mechanism should be added before non-essential cookies or similar technologies are used.
